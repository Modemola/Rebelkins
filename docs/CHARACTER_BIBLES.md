# REBELKIN: THREAD WAR — Character Bibles

> Generated from `src/data/kin.js` and `src/data/shotlists.js` by
> `node tools/build-bibles.mjs`. Edit the data, not this file.

The visual law: **3D from far away, drawn when you lean in. Attitude first.**
Clothes are the kit.

## Shared stat sheet

Every Kin uses the same 8 stats, 1–10.

| Stat | What it decides |
| --- | --- |
| Presence | how hard cameras lock on |
| Ignore | how easy it is to be socially unseen |
| Compile | how fast they rewrite a Thread mid-run |
| Mobility | movement kit |
| Break | fight / style-break power |
| Crowd | how fast civilians copy or protect them |
| Heat Cap | how much fame they can hold before the district slams the door |
| Bond | how well they sync with crew and vehicles |

A **Thread** is a loadout: clothes + item + shoes + hair effect. Swapping a
Thread takes about 3 seconds, scaled by Compile.

## Build order

Do not build twenty characters. Build these six, in this order — it teaches
the game: noise, silence, split attention, stolen identity, escape, crowd magic.

1. **SPARK** — Room-breaker, trend detonator, loud face
2. **SOFT LOCK** — Social stealth, door ghost, anti-camera
3. **THE PAIR** — Decoy + operator, two social roles at once
4. **MIRROR** — Identity thief, packet thief, high-fashion exploit
5. **DRIVER** — Getaway, vehicle bond, living car
6. **FOLK GLITCH** — Crowd conversion, ritual buff, classification error

## SPARK

**True name:** Vex  
**Role:** Room-breaker, trend detonator, loud face  
**Species:** human  
**Subject count:** 1

### Why they exist

The city needs someone who can become the only thing a camera wants to look at. Spark is that problem on purpose.

### Stats

| Presence | Ignore | Compile | Mobility | Break | Crowd | Heat Cap | Bond |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | 2 | 6 | 7 | 9 | 8 | 3 | 5 |

### Voice

Fast, rude, funny, a little too honest. Talks like every sentence could start a fight or a party.

> “Don’t scan me. Watch me.”
> “If the room is quiet, I did it wrong.”
> “Copy the belt. Not the face. The face is mine.”

### Personality / stance

**Ignite** — Forward lean, chin up, weight on the front foot

### Starter Threads

**Thread A — Matchhead**  
Rune vest, chain belt, yellow kicks.  
*Tags:* street, loud · *Access:* 0  
*Effect:* First style-break in a room tags every nearby camera onto Spark for 8s. Crew becomes harder to scan.

**Thread B — Letterbomb**  
Hair clips that spell fragments of a word.  
*Tags:* street, new · *Access:* 0  
*Effect:* Tag a wall. That tag becomes a 12s rally point. Civilians path toward it.

**Thread C — Too Much**  
Maximum color, maximum accessory.  
*Tags:* loud, new · *Access:* 1  
*Effect:* Heat builds twice as fast. Presence becomes a weapon. Doors that hate loud slam. Doors that love new open.

### How they play

Lead with Spark when the mission needs a decoy, a riot, or a bad idea that works. Never leave Spark alone on extraction unless the runway wants chaos.

3. Full body, forward lean, one fist half-raised.

5. Back view, vest runes readable.
6. Shoe hero: yellow or clashing pair, dirty glow.
7. Hair hero: clips and letters isolated on black.
8. Accessory hero: chain belt only.
9. Faces: smug, pissed, delighted, "say it again," mid-shout.
10. Outfit A vest. Outfit B cropped jacket. Outfit C maximal.
11. Neon alley, wet ground, magenta bounce light.
12. Handmade version: cardboard vest, glued sequin runes.
13. Back seat of a car, yelling, window down.
14. Crowd version: 12 civilians already wearing a cheaper copy of the belt.

## SOFT LOCK

**True name:** Nori  
**Role:** Social stealth, door ghost, anti-camera  
**Species:** human / panda-hoodie Kin  
**Subject count:** 1

### Why they exist

Spark makes the room look. Soft Lock walks through the part of the room that stopped looking.

### Stats

| Presence | Ignore | Compile | Mobility | Break | Crowd | Heat Cap | Bond |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 10 | 7 | 6 | 3 | 4 | 8 | 7 |

### Voice

Quiet, dry, short. Not shy in a cute way. Shy like a person who already ran the math.

> “Don’t introduce me.”
> “If they remember my shoes, we failed.”
> “I’ll be at the door. Don’t look for me.”

### Personality / stance

**Avert** — Shoulders in, head down, weight on the back foot

### Starter Threads

**Thread A — Hood Protocol**  
Hood up.  
*Tags:* quiet, street · *Access:* 0  
*Effect:* 6 seconds of "not interesting enough to scan." Breaks if they sprint or attack.

**Thread B — Please Don’t**  
Hands buried, no eye contact.  
*Tags:* quiet · *Access:* 0  
*Effect:* Walking through a crowd does not raise Heat. Standing still near a camera drains that camera’s attention.

**Thread C — Soft Knife**  
Same hoodie, one sharp accessory visible only at close range.  
*Tags:* quiet, luxury · *Access:* 1  
*Effect:* First takedown from unseen does not trigger alarms. Second one does.

### How they play

Soft Lock is the win condition on Ghost Fit and Pair Split missions. They are bad at winning a room and excellent at leaving it.

## The reference wall

### Production rule for all images

- Saturated, but not muddy.
- Outlines visible on inspection.
- Plastic-smooth volumes from far away.
- Imperfect drawn marks up close.
- Personality readable at 64px silhouette.
- No generic anime-pretty faces. Attitude first.

### The 15-plate spine

Every hero Kin gets the same fifteen plates, so a missing plate is visible
instead of forgotten. No character is done until the shoe plate and the
attitude plate both work at thumbnail size.

1. Front portrait, chest-up, neutral light
2. 3/4 portrait, attitude face
3. Full body, standing stance
4. Full body, walking
5. Back view
6. Shoes hero shot
7. Hair hero shot
8. Accessory hero shot
9. Expression sheet
10. Outfit A / B / C
11. Night neon version
12. Handmade / physical-material version
13. In-vehicle version

### Material and drip library

More important than the body:

- oversized hoodies
- chain belts
- rune vests
- plaid / tennis skirts
- fishnets
- chunky sneakers with loud laces
- folk embroidery and beads
- holographic jackets
- animal hoods
- backpacks and handheld items
- star face marks / face paint
- wild multicolor hair with clips and letters

### City and lighting plates

Match the characters, not generic cyberpunk:

- wet purple pavement
- low-angle sneaker shots on bridges
- packed night walkways
- boutique glass
- cardboard/sequin market stalls
- cars with red leather interiors
- floating signage that looks half-illustrated
